01 / THE IDEA

Observe the service, not just the machines

A useful monitoring practice begins with the outcome someone is waiting for. Uptime, request latency and failed work are meaningful because they describe a user experience, not because they fill a dashboard.

Infrastructure numbers help explain a failure. They should not be mistaken for proof that a service is healthy.
