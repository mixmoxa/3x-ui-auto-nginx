Further concepts are explained in the signal model, runbook methods and the interactive budget lab.

Each note begins with a specific operational question and stays within the behaviour it can explain. The material is a public reading reference, not a live service dashboard. For a diagnosis, retain timestamps, distinguish request failures from monitoring gaps and label assumptions that have not been tested. The related runbook material describes how to connect an observation to a reversible first action.

A practical way to use this page is to write down the behaviour described by “field-notes”, then compare it with one observable example. Keep the actual result distinct from the assumption that motivated the test. When something differs, inspect the smallest relevant part before changing other settings.
